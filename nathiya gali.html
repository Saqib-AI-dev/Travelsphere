About Nathia Gali

Nathia Gali is one of the most beautiful hill stations in the Galiyat region of KPK. Surrounded by thick pine forests, cool weather, and breathtaking views of the mountains, it's a perfect weekend getaway from Islamabad and a favorite spot for nature lovers and trekkers.

Quick Facts

Location: Abbottabad District, KPK

Altitude: 2,410 meters

Best Time: May – October

Distance from Islamabad: 90 km (2–3 hours)

Famous For: Pine forests, hiking, Mukeshpuri Top

Top Attractions

Mukeshpuri & Miranjani Trek

Easy-to-moderate hikes with panoramic mountain views.

St. Matthew's Church

Beautiful British-era church in the middle of pine trees.

Pipeline Walking Track

Peaceful 4–5 km forest walk – perfect for families.

Photo Gallery

Best Time to Visit

May to October – Pleasant weather, green forests, perfect for trekking.

July–August: Monsoon brings misty beauty and occasional rain.

How to Reach

  • By Road: 2–3 hours from Islamabad via Murree
  • Public Transport: Buses/Hiace from Rawalpindi & Abbottabad
  • Best Route: Islamabad → Murree → Donga Gali → Nathia Gali

Tips for Visitors

Carry warm jacket even in summer (nights are cold)

Try local corn (bhutta) and tea at roadside stalls

Book hotels in advance during weekends & Eid holidays

Don't miss sunrise from Mukeshpuri Top
