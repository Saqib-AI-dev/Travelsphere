Lahore

The Heart of Pakistan – Badshahi Mosque, Food Street & Rich Culture

Plan Your Trip

About Lahore

Lahore, the cultural capital of Pakistan, is famous for its Mughal architecture, vibrant bazaars, and mouth-watering food. The grand Badshahi Mosque and lively Food Street at night are must-visit experiences that showcase the true soul of the city.

Quick Facts

Location: Punjab, Pakistan

Nickname: City of Gardens

Best Time: October – March

Famous For: Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, Food Street

Airport: Allama Iqbal International

Must-Visit Highlights

Badshahi Mosque

Magnificent red sandstone mosque built in 1673 by Aurangzeb – one of the largest in the world.

Gawalmandi Food Street

Heaven for food lovers – try takatak, hareesa, falooda, and sizzling BBQ under lights.

Lahore Fort & Minar-e-Pakistan

UNESCO site + symbol of Pakistan’s independence.

Photo Gallery

Best Time to Visit

October to March – Pleasant weather, perfect for exploring and enjoying street food at night.

Basanta (spring festival) and winters are the most lively seasons.

How to Reach

  • By Air: Direct flights to Allama Iqbal International Airport
  • By Train: Excellent rail network from Karachi, Islamabad, etc.
  • By Road: Motorway M2 from Islamabad (4 hours)

Food & Shopping Tips

Don’t miss: Butt Karahi, Phajja Siri Paye, Anarkali Bazaar, Liberty Market

Food Street comes alive after 8 PM – best experience on weekends

Try traditional Lahori breakfast: Halwa Puri & Lassi
