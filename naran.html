About Naran

Naran is a scenic hill station in the Kaghan Valley, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, sitting at an altitude of 2,409 meters. Famous for Lake Saif-ul-Malook, it's the gateway to Babusar Pass and a perfect summer retreat.

Quick Facts

Location: Kaghan Valley, Pakistan

Altitude: 2,409 m

Best Time: May – September

Distance from Islamabad: 270 km

Famous For: Saif-ul-Malook Lake

Top Attractions

Lake Saif-ul-Malook

Legendary alpine lake surrounded by glaciers and peaks.

Lulusar Lake

Stunning high-altitude lake on the way to Babusar Pass.

Babusar Pass

4,173m high pass offering breathtaking panoramic views.

Photo Gallery

Best Time to Visit

May to September – Roads open, pleasant weather (10–25°C). Peak season: June–August.

Avoid October–April due to heavy snow.

How to Reach

  • By Road: 6–7 hours from Islamabad via Mansehra
  • Public Transport: Buses/Hiace from Rawalpindi
  • Nearest Airport: Islamabad (ISB)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Naran safe? Yes, very safe and family-friendly.

Do I need a 4x4? Only for Saif-ul-Malook lake, not for Naran town.
