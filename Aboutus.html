Who We Are

We are Adil Abbas and M. Saqib Ali, students of the Artificial Intelligence Department at Aror University.

As part of our final project, our teacher assigned us to create a complete website based on our own choice. After discussing different ideas, we selected a travel guide concept and named our website TravelSphere.

🧭

Our Project: TravelSphere

TravelSphere is a simple, clean, and informative travel guide platform. Our aim is to help travelers explore destinations with clear guidance, travel tips, and essential information.

This project reflects our teamwork, creativity, and learning in web development. We focused on making the website easy to navigate, visually appealing, and useful for all kinds of users.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a platform that inspires people to travel Pakistan with confidence. We want to share guides, safety tips, best places to visit, and travel-friendly suggestions.

As AI students, this project also helped us understand the basics of web development, design principles, responsibility, and teamwork.
